    Questions people ask

    How do I know if a crypto investment is a scam?

    The biggest red flags are guaranteed or very high returns, pressure to act fast, contact that started with a stranger online, and requests to pay in crypto or gift cards. This check covers the 10 most common warning signs.

    What is a pig butchering scam?

    A scammer builds a friendship or romance over weeks, then introduces a "can't lose" crypto platform. Early small withdrawals work to build trust; once you deposit more, the money is gone and new "fees" appear.

    Will a real company ever ask for my seed phrase?

    Never. Your seed phrase (12 or 24 words) is the master key to your wallet. Anyone who has it can take everything. Support staff, exchanges and wallet makers will never ask for it.

    I already sent crypto to a scammer. Can I get it back?

    Crypto transfers are hard to reverse. Report it quickly to your exchange, your bank and the police, and keep all evidence. Be very careful with anyone who promises to recover your funds for a fee: that is a common follow-up scam.
